07 · Layout & motionthe rhythm of the site

Layout

Sticky navbar (pine, z-50) and sticky footer. Full-bleed hero at 50vh max, capped at 600px. Interior sections at max-width 1060–1152px, 24px horizontal padding. Product blocks are full-bleed with brick texture; content is anchored left in a 1/3 · 2/3 split.

Hero rule

No text is overlaid on the hero image. Copy moves into the pine "About" section directly below it. The sign blade speaks; the section that follows explains.

Motion

Deliberate 400–600ms fade-up on scroll for every primary element (headings, product cards, contact form). Motion is architectural, not decorative — one clean reveal per element, never a cascade of micro-interactions.

Brick texture

SVG at 200×80px, 5 courses of thin hairline rectangles at 11% black opacity, tiled. Applied via the .brick-texture utility on every red section. Reference material: aged Pittsburgh PA brick.
